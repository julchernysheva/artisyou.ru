**People / Culture / Technology**

ART.IS.YOU is an independent research platform by **Julia Chernysheva**, Creative R&D Director / Researcher. It investigates how technological systems change human experience, culture, authorship and the boundaries of human agency.

The subject is not technology alone. It is the human transformations that emerge when the systems we create begin to influence how we understand the world and make decisions.

**[Explore ART.IS.YOU ↗](https://artisyou.ru/)**

---

### The research practice

**01 / TECHNOLOGICAL SHIFT** — What becomes possible, and what conditions of human life begin to change?

**02 / ERROR & DISCREPANCY** — Where do technological promises diverge from reality?

**03 / HUMAN SHIFT** — How do roles, habits and human capacities change?

**04 / NEW SOCIETY** — How are cultural norms and collective life reorganized?

**05 / HUMAN MEANING** — What value should technology ultimately create for people?

This is an editorial summary of the research framework presented on the website.

### Selected research systems

**[BOGOBOT](https://artisyou.ru/projects/godbot/)** — Artistic research into what happens to human agency when non-human intelligence participates in knowledge, memory and decisions. [Repository](https://github.com/julchernysheva/bogobot).

**[RUSSIAN INTELLECT](https://artisyou.ru/research/russian-intellect/)** — An interactive atlas of scientific lineages, researchers and technologies. [Repository](https://github.com/julchernysheva/russian-intellect).

**[REVERSE PROMPT](https://artisyou.ru/research/reverse-prompt/)** — An experiment in image interpretation, reverse prompting and machine reconstruction.
